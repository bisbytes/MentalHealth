    Your data

    Your check-ins are stored only in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere and there is no account. Copy your data to keep a backup or move it to another device. This copy is for the app to read back; to share with your therapist, use Share with your therapist above.

    Delete every check-in on this device? This can't be undone.

    Three Circles is a personal journal, not a replacement for care from a professional.

    Based on the three emotion regulation systems (threat, drive and soothing) from Compassion Focused Therapy, developed by Professor Paul Gilbert.
